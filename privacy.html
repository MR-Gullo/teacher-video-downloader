▤ LessonShelf

Privacy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

LessonShelf helps you save videos you have permission to use. This policy describes the extension and its optional Mac companion. The release variants and their permissions are shown during installation.

What the extension uses

The extension reads the title and address of the current non-private tab. It inspects the current page's video elements and recent media manifest addresses to identify the video. It stores your settings and a reference to the last download on your computer. LessonShelf does not support private browsing.

Downloads and the companion

When you choose a browser download, the browser requests the selected address and saves the file using its download settings. The website and its hosting providers receive the request and your network address; the browser may send the site's normal cookies. LessonShelf does not export cookies or authorization headers.

The optional Mac companion receives the current page’s candidate media manifest addresses when you choose to download, and receives the selected link, page title and download settings through native messaging. It may request those manifests from their hosts to identify the current video. It stores the queue and partial files on your computer, including full links needed for retries. Links can contain access tokens. The companion and the download tools contact the sites and servers you choose. The companion does not read browser profiles or export browser cookies. Firefox asks for permission before sending selected browsing activity and website content to the companion; direct browser downloads remain available if you decline. If you later revoke permission, the extension stops communicating with the companion. Previously submitted companion jobs may continue; pause them before revoking permission if you want them to stop.

Sharing and retention

LessonShelf has no account, advertising, analytics or developer-operated download service. It does not send your queue to the developer. Websites, their service providers, and the tools you install have their own policies. Your browser keeps its own download history. The companion retains queue history, downloads and partial files until you remove them deliberately; uninstalling the companion keeps that data. See the Mac setup guide before removing files you may need.

Questions or corrections: contact the project through GitHub. Do not include private links, student information or files in a public issue.
