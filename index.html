Optional Mac companion for streams and conversion

Direct files can be saved by your browser. HLS streams, the local queue, and conversion need a separate companion and tools on Mac. The extension does not include them. The companion is not available on Windows.

Read the Mac companion setup guide

A few things to know

Save only videos you have permission to use. Sites can block downloads, and protected or DRM media is not supported. The extension does not export browser cookies. Browser downloads may send the normal cookies for the requested site. Direct file downloads use your browser on Windows and Mac. Page downloads, queue recovery, and conversion need the optional Mac companion and its tools; they are not available on Windows.
